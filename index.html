Travel Agency App — Complete User Guide

The standard operating guide for our travel-agency booking & billing application. The workflow is the same across every edition we deploy — only the branding, logo and web address change per agency.

Each agency uses its own web address (for example your-agency.web.app). Open your app's link, add it to your home screen, and follow the flows below.

1. About this guide

This guide is common to all travel apps we build. Every edition shares the same screens and the same step-by-step flows described here; the only differences between agencies are the name, logo, invoice header and the web address you open. Wherever this guide says "the app" or "your agency", it means your own edition.

The app runs in any modern browser on phone, tablet or computer. It works like a normal website — no installation needed. For quick access, open your app link and choose Add to Home Screen.

Your data is always safe. Adding new features never changes or deletes what you already entered — existing clients, bookings, bills and payments keep working exactly as before.

2. Logging in & roles

  1. Open your agency's app link in a browser.
  2. Enter your registered phone number and password.
  3. Tap Login. You'll land on the Home dashboard.

If you forget your password, an admin can reset it for you (section 22).

Roles

RoleAccess
StaffClients, Providers, B2B Agents, Bookings, Bills, Payments, Packages, Attestation. Profit and margin figures are hidden.
AdminEverything staff can do, plus Cash/Bank Accounts, Office Expenses, Reports, Staff Activity, Activity Log and User Management. Admins also see profit/margin everywhere.
Sections marked Admin are visible only to admins.

3. Navigating the app

  • On desktop, use the sidebar on the left to move between screens.
  • On mobile, tap the menu (☰) or use the bottom navigation.
  • Lists load more as you scroll. Most lists have a search box and, where useful, a date range filter.
  • Add something with the Add button (usually top-right). Tap any row to open it, then Edit or Delete from there.
Delete protection: the app won't let you delete a record that others depend on (for example a client or provider that still has bookings). Remove or reassign those first.

4. Home dashboard

The Home screen summarises the business: totals for bookings, outstanding client balances, and quick charts. Admins also see profit summaries. Use it as your daily starting point.

5. Clients

Clients are the customers you bill.

Add a client

  1. Open Clients → Add Client.
  2. Enter Name and Phone (required), plus optional alt phone, email, address.
  3. If they owed money before using the app, enter it as Opening Balance.
  4. Tap Save.

Edit / delete a client

  1. Tap the client to open them.
  2. Tap Edit, change details, Save. (Editing the opening balance can't drop it below what's already been paid against it.)
  3. To remove, tap Delete — allowed only if the client has no bookings/bills.

Client statement & payments

  1. Open the client to see their statement: bills, payments and running balance.
  2. Tap Record Payment to log money received (section 10).
  3. Use Print to produce a PDF statement (with bank details and QR where configured) to print or share.

6. Providers / Suppliers

Providers are your suppliers — airlines, bus/train operators, hotels, consolidators, visa agents, medical agents, forex/remittance, purchase suppliers, etc.

Add a provider

  1. Open Providers → Add Provider.
  2. Enter the name and pick one or more service types (a provider can supply several, e.g. Bus + Train).
  3. Optionally add contact person, phone, email, GSTIN.
  4. If you already owed them money, enter it as Opening Balance (dues brought in).
  5. Tap Save.

Provider balance

Every provider shows a running balance made of: Opening + Purchases − Paid.

  • Purchases build up automatically from the cost price of every booking taken from them.
  • Paid is what you've paid them via Record Payment.
  • A positive balance means you owe them; zero or negative means settled / they owe you.

Record a payment to a provider

  1. Open the provider → Record Payment.
  2. Enter amount and date, then pick the Cash/Bank account it's paid from (the available balance is shown).
  3. Save. Paying more than that account holds is blocked.

Payment / credit history

  1. Open the provider → History to see every credit and payment.
  2. Tap an entry to edit or delete it (corrections are safe — balances recompute).

Provider statement

Open a provider and use Statement / Print for a settlement statement over any date range.

How supplier debt works: the full cost price of a booking is what you owe the supplier. Refunding the client does not reduce what you owe the supplier — only an actual supplier credit or payment does.

7. B2B Agents

B2B Agents are other travel businesses you trade with — in both directions.

Add a B2B agent

  1. Open B2B Agents → Add Agent.
  2. Enter name, phone and optional details; enter an Opening Balance for any prior dues.
  3. Save.

Agent as a customer (you sell to them)

When creating a booking, set Booking for → B2B Agent. The bill goes to the agent and appears on their statement — kept entirely separate from normal client statements.

Agent as a supplier (you buy from them)

On a client booking, set Supplier → B2B Agent. You then owe that agent the booking's cost. Pay them out from the agent screen, choosing the account.

Netted settlement & statement

  • Each agent shows a single net balance = what they owe you − what you owe them.
  • Open an agent for a printable statement showing both the receivable and payable sides.
  • Record Payout to pay an agent (from a chosen account); record a credit when they owe you back.

8. Bookings

A booking is one service sold. Open Bookings → Add Booking.

Step-by-step

  1. Choose the Booking type: Flight, Bus, Train, Hotel, Visa, or Medical Appointment.
  2. Booking for: choose Client or B2B Agent, then pick who.
  3. Supplier: choose Provider or B2B Agent, then pick who supplies it. (The supplier list is filtered to types that match the booking.)
  4. Fill the type-specific fields (see below).
  5. Enter Cost Price (what you pay the supplier) and Selling Price (what you bill). Admins see the resulting profit.
  6. Set the Booking date and any notes, then Save.

Fields by booking type

TypeKey fields
Flight / Bus / Train / HotelRoute, passenger name(s), PNR, travel date
VisaVisa type (in the route field), passport number (in the PNR field), travel date
Medical AppointmentTravel Country (instead of route/PNR/travel date); supplied by a Medical Agent

Edit / delete a booking

  1. Open the booking → Edit, change fields, Save.
  2. Delete removes it (and is recorded in the Activity log).

Generate the bill

From a booking, tap Generate Bill to create its invoice. Each booking can have only one bill; the number is assigned automatically.

9. Bills & invoices

The Bills screen lists every invoice with its party (a Client or a B2B Agent tag), amount and paid/pending status.

Actions on a bill

  • Print — a clean invoice with your business header, seal, and bank transfer details for payment.
  • Record Payment — section 10.
  • Send via WhatsApp — section 12.
  • Refund — section 11 (for cancelled bookings).

Opening-balance bills

A client's or agent's brought-forward due appears as a special OB- numbered bill so it flows through balances and payments like any other.

10. Recording payments

Every time money comes in or goes out, you choose the Cash/Bank account it moved through — this keeps account balances accurate and gives real statements.

Record money received (from a client/agent)

  1. From a bill or the client/agent, tap Record Payment.
  2. Enter the amount and date.
  3. Pick the account the money went into.
  4. Save. Over-payment (more than the amount due) is blocked automatically. A payment across several bills is split for you.

Correct or delete a payment

  1. Open the payment history (on the client/agent).
  2. Tap the receipt to edit the amount/date/account, or delete it — affected bills revert automatically.
If no accounts exist yet, the app still shows a simple method list (Cash/UPI/Bank Transfer/Other) so payments are never blocked. Create accounts (section 13) to unlock balance tracking.

11. Refunds & cancellations

  1. Open the booking (or its bill) → Refund.
  2. Enter the amount refunded to the client; any difference from the selling price is kept as a penalty (it becomes the bill's new amount).
  3. Choose the account the refund is paid from.
  4. Save. If the booking was supplied by a B2B agent, the supplier side is reconciled automatically.

12. Sending invoices on WhatsApp

  1. Open a bill → 💬 Send via WhatsApp.
  2. The app builds the invoice as a PDF. On a phone it opens the share sheet; on a desktop it downloads the PDF and opens the WhatsApp chat for the client's number.
  3. On desktop, attach the downloaded PDF and send — the message (amount, balance, invoice number) is pre-filled.
The customer's phone number must be on the client/agent record for WhatsApp to open the right chat.

13. Cash & Bank accounts Admin

Accounts are the cash drawer and bank accounts your money flows through. Open Accounts.

Add a bank account

  1. Open Accounts → Bank tab → Add Account.
  2. Enter the account name, bank name, account number and IFSC; optionally an opening balance.
  3. Mark one bank as Default — its details print on invoices.
  4. Save.

Cash is built in and always present; only banks are added. An account with recorded transactions can't be deleted (reassign/remove those first).

View an account statement

  1. Open the account.
  2. See its live balance and full list of money in/out (payments, expenses, provider/agent payouts, package receipts, transfers).
  3. Set a date range and Print the statement.

14. Transfers Admin

  1. Open Accounts → Transfer.
  2. Choose the from and to accounts (must be different) and the amount.
  3. Save. It shows as money out of one account and into the other. Transferring more than the source holds is blocked.

15. Supplier & agent settlements

"Settlement" means clearing what you owe (or are owed by) a supplier or agent.

  • Providers: each provider's balance is Opening + Purchases − Paid. Record payments to reduce it; open the provider for a printable settlement statement.
  • B2B Agents: a single netted balance across both directions (you sold to them / you bought from them). Record payouts or credits; print the agent statement.
  • Packages: costs recorded against a vendor inside a package also roll into that vendor's balance here, so all settlement lives in one place.

16. Office expenses Admin

  1. Open Expenses → Add Expense.
  2. Enter the category (or a custom one), amount and date.
  3. Pick the account it's paid from (reduces that account's balance).
  4. Save. Edit or delete any expense later; print an expense report for a period.

17. Packages / Group tours

The Packages module runs full trip packages (Umra, Hajj, group tours, custom trips) end to end.

Create a package (event)

  1. Open Packages → Add Package.
  2. Enter title, trip type (preset or custom), start/end dates, capacity (max seats) and travel modes.
  3. Save, then open it to work through its sections.

Sections inside a package

SectionHow to use it
PassengersAdd travellers one by one, or as a group (family/party). Set each passenger's package price. Capacity is enforced against the seats you set.
PaymentsRecord a passenger's payment (choose the account). Use Group Payment to collect one amount and split it across a group automatically.
Transport / Hotels / PurchasesRecord each cost against a vendor (a provider or B2B agent), with mobile/remarks. These feed the settlement.
ExpensesPackage expenses — Visa costs go to a vendor; other expenses are paid from an account.
InvoicesGenerate a multi-passenger invoice (one per family/group); print or share it.
SettlementPer-vendor breakdown: agreed cost, paid, recovered, balance.
Overview AdminPackage value, collected, total cost and profit.

Refund / cancel a passenger

  1. Open the passenger → Refund.
  2. Enter the refund amount and any penalty kept; choose the account.
  3. Save — the seat is freed and any supplier recovery is captured for the settlement.

18. Attestation

The Attestation screen manages certificate/document attestation jobs.

  1. Open Attestation → Add.
  2. Pick the client, the supplier (provider or B2B agent), the country and category (preset or custom), and the cost/selling price.
  3. Save — it creates a client bill like any booking.
  4. Update the status inline as the job progresses: Processing → In Transfer → Approved / Rejected → In Transfer Back → Completed.

19. Reports Admin

The Reports screen gives admins the big picture over any date range: a per-booking Profit Report, provider settlement totals (including package vendors), and printable summaries.

20. Staff Activity Admin

Staff Activity shows how much each staff member created — bookings, packages and profit generated — over a chosen date range. Records made before this feature, or by admins, appear as "unattributed".

21. Activity log Admin

The Activity screen is a complete audit trail: every create, edit and delete across the app (bookings, bills, payments, clients, providers, accounts, transfers, B2B agents, packages and more) with who did it and when. Filter by user, type or date.

22. User management Admin

  1. Open Manage Users → Add User.
  2. Enter name, phone, a password and the role (staff/admin).
  3. Save. Later you can reset a password or deactivate a user from their row.

23. Profile & password

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Review your details and tap Change Password.
  3. Enter your current and new password, then save. Use a strong password and keep it private.

25. FAQ & tips

Why does the payment form ask for an account, not a method?

Choosing the real Cash/Bank account keeps balances correct and enables statements. If you haven't set up accounts, the old method list still appears.

A supplier's balance looks different after an update.

Balances use the improved model (full cost owed until you actually pay/credit the supplier). Your records are unchanged — only the calculation improved. Set each supplier's Opening Balance for dues carried in.

Can a booking be for another agency?

Yes — set Booking for → B2B Agent. It's billed to the agent and kept off client statements.

How do I stop someone deleting important data?

The app already blocks deleting a client/provider/agent that still has bookings, and every change is recorded in the Activity log.

Is my old data safe when the app is updated?

Yes. Updates are additive — existing clients, bookings, bills and payments continue to work unchanged.
